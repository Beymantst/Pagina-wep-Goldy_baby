The Problem: Sensitive Skin + Bad Clothing

Many times, we see babies and children with skin conditions and even respiratory infections caused by clothing that has not been properly treated, dyed with aggressive chemicals, or made with synthetic blends. For babies, whose skin is 5x thinner than adults, this is critical.

In Colombia, local cotton production is reducing and not enough to meet demand for high-quality hypoallergenic textiles. The industry needs external sourcing to maintain quality, volume and price stability. Parents end up paying more for imported finished garments that are still not guaranteed hypoallergenic.

Our solution: GoldyBaby S.A.S. creates pajamas and baby clothes that are 100% cotton, soft, natural and hypoallergenic. Our main element is cotton — pure, organic, with quality controls without aggressive chemicals, with sustainability certifications.

Colombia Context — Why Import?

Cotton production in Colombia has decreased significantly over the last decade. Not enough volume, inconsistent quality, higher costs. To produce hypoallergenic clothing at scale with stable supply, we must look to Brazil — world's second largest exporter, with proximity that reduces logistics costs and delivery times, plus organic and sustainability certifications that match GoldyBaby values.

ACE-72 Big Win

0% vs 40% tariff difference = huge saving for GoldyBaby

Under ACE-72 (Economic Complementation Agreement between Colombia, Ecuador, Venezuela and MERCOSUR), cotton from Brazil enters at 0% tariff with proper Certificate of Origin. Without agreement, general tariff is up to 40%. For a startup making pajamas for sensitive skin, this saving allows competitive pricing while keeping quality.

Legal & Historical Note

Colombia after independence 1810, Gran Colombia 1831, Constitution 1886 centralizing state, then modern Constitution 1991 opened foreign trade, created DIAN (tax & customs), MinCIT (Commerce Ministry), VUCE (single window). Brazil similar trade opening. This framework enables GoldyBaby import today. Understanding this history justifies why ACE-72 exists and why DIAN/VUCE procedures matter.

Historical Legal Framework

Colombia 1810 independence → Gran Colombia 1831 → Constitution 1886 → 1991 Constitution opened foreign trade → Creation of DIAN, MinCIT, VUCE. This evolution created modern import law. GoldyBaby must comply with this framework. Brazil has similar MERCOSUR integration that led to ACE-72.
